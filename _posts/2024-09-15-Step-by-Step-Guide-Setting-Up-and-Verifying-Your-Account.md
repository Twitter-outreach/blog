# Step-by-Step Guide: Setting Up and Verifying Your Account

This guide provides essential steps for setting up and securing user accounts, ensuring verification, and enhancing engagement on X (Twitter) using Xreacher.

## 1. Account Verification and Setup

- Wait for verification eligibility:
  - Wait 3 days before verifying your account.
- Verify phone number:
  - Use a service like [SMSpva](https://smspva.com/?ref=753126) to verify phone numbers for multiple accounts.
- Upgrade to premium:
  - Upgrade to the X Premium for US$8/mo.
- Use different emails and virtual cards:
  - Use different email addresses for each account.
  - Utilize different virtual cards for each account (e.g., Revolut, Wise, or Bunq).

## 2. Billing Information

- Enter cardholder name:
  - Can use the account name as the cardholder name.
- Use a random address:
  - Enter a random address for billing purposes.
  - Consider tax rates when selecting an address.
- Subscribe and save card info:
  - Complete the subscription and save the card information.

## 3. Profile Setup

- Follow users:
  - Follow 100-300 people.
- Engage with content:
  - Like at least 10-20 posts.
  - Repost 20-40 posts or create original content.
- Pin important tweets:
  - Pin significant tweets to the top of your profile.
- Wait for verification:
  - Wait approximately three days for the verification checkmark.

## 4. Account Security and Suspension Prevention

- Replace proxy if banned:
  - Use a new proxy if your account gets banned.
- Use proxies for new accounts:
  - Always use a new proxy when logging into a new account.
- Profile photo guidelines:
  - Avoid AI-generated profile pictures.
  - Use a photo of a real person from social media for your profile photo.
  - Better to use a photo of a person for profile photo over a brand logo.

## 5. Account Warm-up

- Message interactions:
  - Message 3-4 people to warm up the account (up to 5-6 for safety).
